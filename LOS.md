## 1. Ethical & Professional Standards — weight 5–10%

### 1.1 Code of Ethics & Standards of Professional Conduct (Guidance) 🟡
- Describe the structure of the CFA Institute Professional Conduct Program and the disciplinary process.
- Explain the ethical responsibilities required by the Code of Ethics and the seven Standards (I–VII), including sub-sections.
- Recommend practices and procedures that support compliance with the Standards.
- Identify conduct that conforms to, or violates, the Code and Standards.

### 1.2 Application of the Code and Standards: Level II 🟡
- Evaluate professional conduct in complex, multi-issue scenarios and determine which Standards apply.
- Explain how the Code and Standards resolve conflicts in realistic case settings.

### 1.3 Global Investment Performance Standards (GIPS) 🟡
- Explain the objectives, key characteristics, and scope of GIPS, and who can claim compliance.
- Describe the fundamentals of compliance, input data, composite construction, and required disclosures.
- Describe presentation, reporting, and verification requirements.

_Topic subtotal ≈ 9 LOS · app: 42 flashcards (10 groups), 2 item sets (9 Qs)_

## 2. Quantitative Methods — weight 5–10%

### 2.1 Basics of Multiple Regression & Underlying Assumptions ✅
- [a] Describe the types of investment problems addressed by multiple linear regression and formulate the model.
- [b] Interpret the relationship between the dependent variable and the multiple independent variables (partial slope coefficients).
- [c] Explain the assumptions underlying the multiple regression model.
- [d] Interpret diagnostic plots / residuals used to check the assumptions.
- [f] Calculate the predicted value of the dependent variable for assumed values of the independent variables.

### 2.2 Evaluating Regression Model Fit & Interpreting Results ✅
- Evaluate goodness of fit using R², adjusted R², and information criteria (AIC, BIC).
- Formulate and test hypotheses on individual coefficients (t-test, p-value, confidence interval).
- Formulate and test a joint hypothesis on coefficients (F-test, general linear F-test).
- Interpret the ANOVA table.

### 2.3 Model Misspecification ✅
- Describe common types of model misspecification and their consequences.
- Explain, detect, and correct heteroskedasticity, serial correlation, and multicollinearity.

### 2.4 Extensions of Multiple Regression 🟡
- Formulate and interpret models with dummy (indicator) and interaction variables.
- Describe logistic regression, interpret logit coefficients, and evaluate a logistic model.

### 2.5 Time-Series Analysis 🟡
- Calculate/evaluate trend models; explain limitations.
- Explain AR models, mean reversion, and forecasting; evaluate with RMSE.
- Explain unit roots / random walks and first-differencing; seasonality; ARCH; regression with more than one time series (cointegration).

### 2.6 Machine Learning 🟡
- Distinguish supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning; describe overfitting and its prevention.
- Describe penalized regression (LASSO), SVM, KNN, CART, ensemble/random forest, and neural nets / deep learning / RL.
- Describe clustering (k-means, hierarchical) and dimension reduction (PCA).

### 2.7 Big Data Projects ✅
- Identify and describe the steps in building ML models for structured and unstructured (text) data.
- Describe data collection/curation, preparation & wrangling, and exploration for both data types.
- Describe feature selection/engineering and model training, and evaluate model performance (classification metrics).

_Topic subtotal ≈ 24 LOS · app: 50 flashcards (7 modules), 4 item sets (19 Qs), 2 interactive tools_

## 3. Economics — weight 5–10%

### 3.1 Currency Exchange Rates: Understanding Equilibrium Value 🟡
- Calculate/interpret cross rates and forward premiums/discounts; mark-to-market of forwards.
- Explain covered and uncovered interest rate parity, PPP, and the international Fisher effect.
- Describe the carry trade and its risks; explain balance-of-payments, monetary/fiscal policy (Mundell-Fleming), and capital flows effects on exchange rates.

### 3.2 Economic Growth 🟡
- Explain preconditions for and sources of economic growth; the production function and growth accounting.
- Explain convergence hypotheses and the neoclassical (Solow) and endogenous growth models.

### 3.3 Economics of Regulation 🟡
- Describe classifications of regulation and the rationale for regulation.
- Describe regulatory tools, regulatory capture, and the costs/benefits and effects on business.

_Topic subtotal ≈ 12 LOS · app: 33 flashcards (3 modules), 2 item sets (8 Qs)_

## 4. Financial Statement Analysis — weight 10–15%

### 4.1 Intercorporate Investments 🟡
- Classify and account for investments in financial assets, associates (equity method), and business combinations (acquisition method).
- Analyze the effects of different classifications and of SPEs/VIEs on financial statements.

### 4.2 Employee Compensation: Post-Employment & Share-Based 🟡
- Describe DB vs DC pensions; measure and analyze the net pension asset/liability and periodic cost (IFRS vs US GAAP).
- Explain and analyze share-based compensation.

### 4.3 Multinational Operations 🟡
- Distinguish functional/presentation currency; compare the current-rate (translation) and temporal (remeasurement) methods.
- Analyze the effects of each method and of hyperinflation on financial statements and ratios.

### 4.4 Analysis of Financial Institutions 🟡
- Describe how the analysis of banks and insurers differs from other companies (e.g., CAMELS).
- Analyze a financial institution's financial statements.

### 4.5 Evaluating Quality of Financial Reports 🟡
- Describe the quality spectrum of financial reports; distinguish earnings, cash flow, and balance-sheet quality.
- Describe indicators/warning signs of low-quality or manipulated reporting.

### 4.6 Integration of Financial Statement Analysis Techniques 🟡
- Apply integrated FSA techniques to evaluate performance, quality, and valuation across companies.

_Topic subtotal ≈ 16 LOS · app: 54 flashcards (6 modules), 3 item sets (15 Qs)_

## 5. Corporate Issuers — weight 5–10%

### 5.1 Analysis of Dividends & Share Repurchases 🟡
- Describe dividend theories and types; compare cash dividends vs share repurchases and their effects.
- Analyze dividend safety (payout, coverage) and calculate the effect of repurchases on EPS/BVPS.

### 5.2 ESG Considerations in Investment Analysis 🟡
- Describe ESG integration and the use of ESG factors in valuation and risk analysis.

### 5.3 Cost of Capital: Advanced Topics 🟡
- Explain the top-down and bottom-up drivers of the cost of capital; estimate cost of equity/debt.
- Incorporate country risk and flotation costs; compute WACC in advanced settings.

### 5.4 Corporate Restructuring 🟡
- Describe forms of restructuring (M&A, divestitures); explain motivations and evaluate transactions and valuations.

_Topic subtotal ≈ 10 LOS · app: 27 flashcards (4 modules), 1 item set (5 Qs) — ⬜ item-set light_

## 6. Equity Valuation — weight 10–15%

### 6.1 Equity Valuation: Applications & Processes 🟡
- Describe the valuation process, categories of models, and criteria for choosing a model.

### 6.2 Return Concepts 🟡
- Distinguish required return, expected return, and discount rate; estimate the equity risk premium.
- Estimate required return using CAPM, multifactor models, and build-up methods.

### 6.3 Industry & Company Analysis (Forecasting) 🟡
- Forecast revenue, margins, and financial results; build pro-forma statements.

### 6.4 Discounted Dividend Valuation ✅
- Apply the DDM: Gordon growth, multistage, and H-model; estimate sustainable growth.

### 6.5 Free Cash Flow Valuation ✅
- Compute FCFF and FCFE; value equity with single- and multi-stage FCF models.

### 6.6 Market-Based Valuation (Price & EV Multiples) 🟡
- Compute and interpret P/E, P/B, P/S, P/CF, and EV multiples; apply method of comparables and justified multiples.

### 6.7 Residual Income Valuation ✅
- Compute residual income and value equity; relate RI to other models; discuss continuing residual income.

### 6.8 Private Company Valuation 🟡
- Apply income, market, and asset-based approaches; apply discounts (DLOM, DLOC).

_Topic subtotal ≈ 18 LOS · app: 65 flashcards (8 modules), 3 item sets (13 Qs)_

## 7. Fixed Income — weight 10–15%

### 7.1 The Term Structure & Interest Rate Dynamics 🟡
- Calculate spot, forward, and par rates and the swap rate curve; describe term-structure theories and key drivers.

### 7.2 The Arbitrage-Free Valuation Framework ✅
- Value bonds with binomial interest-rate trees (backward induction) and pathwise/Monte Carlo methods.

### 7.3 Valuation & Analysis of Bonds with Embedded Options ✅
- Value callable/putable bonds and compute OAS; compute effective duration/convexity; value convertibles.

### 7.4 Credit Analysis Models 🟡
- Explain structural and reduced-form credit models; analyze credit spreads and the term structure of credit.

### 7.5 Credit Default Swaps 🟡
- Describe CDS mechanics and pricing; explain applications in portfolio and relative-value strategies.

### 7.6 Mortgage-Backed & Asset-Backed Securities 🟡 _(verify placement/year)_
- Describe MBS/ABS structures, prepayment risk, and valuation considerations.

_Topic subtotal ≈ 14 LOS · app: 54 flashcards (6 modules), 2 item sets (10 Qs)_

## 8. Derivatives — weight 5–10%

### 8.1 Pricing & Valuation of Forward Commitments ✅
- Price and value forwards, futures, and swaps (interest-rate, currency, equity) using no-arbitrage.

### 8.2 Valuation of Contingent Claims ✅
- Value options with binomial models and Black–Scholes–Merton; interpret the Greeks and dynamic hedging.

## 9. Alternative Investments — weight 5–10%

### 9.1 Commodities & Commodity Derivatives 🟡
- Explain commodity sectors and pricing; describe roll yield and theories of futures returns (contango/backwardation).

### 9.2 Real Estate Investments 🟡
- Describe private and public real estate; apply income, cost, and comparable-sales approaches; analyze REITs.

### 9.3 Investments in Private Capital (Private Equity) 🟡
- Describe private equity structures, fees, and exit routes; evaluate fund performance (IRR, multiples, J-curve).

### 9.4 Hedge Fund Strategies 🟡
- Describe major hedge-fund strategies and their risk/return; evaluate their role in a portfolio.

## 10. Portfolio Management — weight 10–15%

### 10.1 Economics & Investment Markets 🟡
- Explain how economic factors and the discount-rate framework drive the valuation of asset classes.

### 10.2 Analysis of Active Portfolio Management ✅
- Explain value added and the information ratio; apply the fundamental law of active management.

### 10.3 Exchange-Traded Funds: Mechanics & Applications 🟡
- Explain ETF creation/redemption, tracking error, and portfolio applications.

### 10.4 Using Multifactor Models 🟡
- Explain arbitrage pricing theory and multifactor models; interpret factor sensitivities and active risk.

### 10.5 Measuring & Managing Market Risk ✅
- Compute and interpret VaR (parametric, historical, Monte Carlo); describe sensitivity and scenario measures; stress testing.

### 10.6 Backtesting & Simulation 🟡
- Describe backtesting steps and pitfalls; explain historical vs Monte Carlo simulation and sensitivity analysis.

### 10.7 Trading Costs & Electronic Markets 🟡 _(verify placement/year)_
- Describe trading costs, market structure, and electronic/algorithmic trading.
